Titanym

SiGe-on-sapphire process reproduction and qualification.

Titanym is a technical program focused on reproducing and evaluating a low-temperature silicon-germanium growth process on sapphire substrates.

Phase 1 is designed to determine whether the process can be reliably reproduced and whether the resulting material meets the quality requirements for future device work.

Program Overview

Titanym begins with
materials qualification.

The program attempts to reproduce reported SiGe-on-sapphire growth conditions in a controlled nanofabrication environment and measure the resulting crystal material.

This phase is not focused on device deployment.

The objective is to determine whether the process can produce repeatable, high-quality semiconductor material suitable for further development.

Scope

Phase 1 Scope

Phase 1 focuses on a tightly scoped set of process and measurement objectives.

Process Reproduction

Reproducing low-temperature SiGe growth conditions from reported literature parameters.

Crystal Quality

Measuring crystal quality, orientation, and structural integrity of deposited SiGe layers.

Defect Mapping

Mapping defect structures using X-ray diffraction — dislocations, twin defects, contamination.

Wafer Stress

Evaluating wafer stress through curvature analysis and thermal load performance testing.

Surface Condition

AFM and microscopy analysis of surface roughness and defect counts across the wafer.

Repeatability

Testing reproducibility across multiple growth runs with statistical analysis and clear Go/No-Go criteria.

Program Output

Qualification report and Go / No-Go decision for subsequent device work.

Evaluation Sequence

The Qualification Path

The program follows a defined evaluation sequence — each step gates the next.

01

Reproduce Growth Conditions

Reproduce reported low-temperature SiGe growth conditions in a controlled nanofabrication environment.

02

Characterize Crystal Quality

Single crystal confirmation, SiGe orientation alignment, and X-ray diffraction defect density mapping.

03

Evaluate Repeatability

Statistical analysis across multiple growth runs. Surface condition, wafer stress, and defect uniformity.

04

Produce Qualification Report

Document all measurement data, process parameters, and material characterization findings.

05

Issue Go / No-Go Decision

A formal determination on whether the material system is ready for subsequent device development work.

Qualification first. Device development later.
Measurement Framework

SiGe-on-Sapphire Wafer Qualification

Crystal Quality

  • Single crystal confirmation
  • SiGe orientation alignment

Defect Structure

  • X-ray diffraction for dislocations
  • Twin defects, contamination

Wafer Stress

  • Curvature analysis for internal stress
  • Thermal load performance

Surface Condition

  • AFM and microscopy analysis
  • Defect counts, surface roughness

Repeatability

  • Statistical analysis of multiple wafer runs
  • Clear Go / No-Go criteria
Go / No-Go

Each qualification metric must pass before a Go decision is issued.
